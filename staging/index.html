Featured project

The lab I'm most proud of so far.

Closed — findings documented

Internal Certificate Authority & TLS Hardening

I stood up an internal Certificate Authority from scratch and used it to issue and deploy TLS certificates across a lab environment, then went back through the setup looking for misconfigurations — weak cipher suites, certificate chain issues, expired or self-signed certs being trusted incorrectly. Working through both sides, building it and then trying to break it, gave me a much clearer picture of how PKI actually fails in practice, not just how it's supposed to work.
